# Torii Gates

Torii gates are where a mage's education begins. They generate in the world like any other structure, and
`/locate structure isekubed:torii_gate` finds one.

Right-click a gate to learn **DISCOVER**, your first circle. A gate you find in the world also teaches one more word of
its own. Different gates teach different words, so it is worth visiting more than one.

**A found gate is fragile.** Break any one of its blocks and the whole gate vanishes. You can also build a gate
yourself. A built gate teaches DISCOVER but no word of its own.

Every gate has an **offering barrel** between its posts. The right offering, followed by a cast on the barrel, is
answered. What a gate wants is for you to find out.
