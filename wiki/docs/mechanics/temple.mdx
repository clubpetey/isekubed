# The Temple

The temple is where the final boss waits. It is a dimension of its own, and it is not for the unprepared.

## Getting there

You need to know DISCOVER. Then you need a fast minecart, like one on powered rails. A cart that runs into you at speed
carries you into the temple (`minecart_door_speed_centiblocks`, default 38, is how fast "fast" is, in hundredths of a
block per tick). You cannot be riding when it happens, and you cannot go from inside the Trial.

## Inside

- You arrive at one of the temple's gates.
- Each gate has a pad. Stand on it and you are carried home.
- If you fall, you are sent home with full health.
- Seraphelle waits.
- The island refuses circles. Its ground takes no circle, and you cannot place or break blocks on it, or change it with
  buckets, fire or explosions.

Bring everything. Your gear, your spells and your soul are all tested.

The ending is yours to find. There are two endings. Or three.
