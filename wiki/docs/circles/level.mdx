# Level

The Level circles are a ladder of eight rungs. Each one you complete opens the next, and the rungs from II up carry the name of a sin. Climbing it teaches you the numerals that unlock higher levels of other circles. Working out what each rung wants is part of the climb.

A Level circle does nothing on its own and costs nothing to leave drawn. You pay once, when you draw it, and the price climbs with each rung.

| Circle | Mana (cast) |
|---|---|
| Level I | 200 |
| Level II · Gluttony | 300 |
| Level III · Lust | 500 |
| Level IV · Sloth | 800 |
| Level V · Envy | 1300 |
| Level VI · Greed | 2100 |
| Level VII · Wrath | 3400 |
| Level VIII · Pride | 5500 |
