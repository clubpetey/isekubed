# RF

The bridge between mana and other mods' energy (RF). The level sets how far the circle reaches: level 1 works on its own block, level 2 on the 3x3 around it, and so on up to level 9.

- **RF Split** turns RF into mana. Draw it on a battery, generator or machine that can give energy out and it drains that block, and every energy source in its reach, into your mana network. It also makes a trickle of RF by itself from certain blocks in its reach, such as a redstone block or ancient debris, without using them up. It never takes more than your network can hold, and it needs a network to feed: a mana gem in a network bag is the smallest one. A red face usually means you are on an input side of the block, or the network is full.
- **RF Merge** turns mana into RF. It pushes energy out of your network into whatever is in reach that will accept it, such as batteries and machines, and goes red when nothing will take any.

Higher levels widen the reach and raise the upkeep.
