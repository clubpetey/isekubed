# Cure

Removes every harmful effect from you or from a target, the soul curses included (they can return later, so it is a reprieve, not immunity). It also ends a Full Cowl or a Field. Aimed at a block or at nothing, it cures you.

Cure I is instant and nothing more. From Cure II up it also leaves you protected for a while afterwards, 20 seconds per level, during which harmful effects are refused as they land and swept off every tick. Higher levels buy time, not strength, and the mana cost stays flat.
