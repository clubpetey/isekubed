# Repair

Mends damaged items. Draw it on top of a pedestal, a chest or any other block, and put the damaged items on the pedestal, in the chest, or on the circle itself. It repairs them point by point, and you only pay mana for damage actually fixed, so a circle left sitting on a chest of undamaged gear costs nothing. If you cannot afford it all at once, it mends what you can pay for and carries on later.

Pedestals and other repair apparatus make it cheaper: anywhere else costs twice the mana. It has a single level.
