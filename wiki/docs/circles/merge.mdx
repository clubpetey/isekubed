# Merge

Takes items out of your storage network. Right-click an item onto the circle to set a filter; the item is not used up. The circle then pulls that item out of the network into the inventory it is drawn on, and into every inventory in its reach. Pinned to a filled fluid container, it fills the tanks, cauldrons and machines it covers.

With no inventory it drops the items into the world, but only where they can fall: on a bottom face over open space, or on a mana block.

Each level widens the reach. A higher level costs less per block covered than a cluster of small circles.
