# Split

Puts things into your storage network. Items resting on the circle go in as themselves. Draw it on a chest, cauldron or machine and it drains what is inside into the network.

It also harvests. Over mature crops it takes the produce and leaves the seed, and over a tree it cuts the tree down and replants it. Over a full beehive it takes the honey and honeycombs. Pin a filled bucket to a circle on a floor or ceiling and it drills down through source blocks of that fluid; water is infinite. [Growth]($circles/growth) plus Split is a crop farm.

Each level widens the disc, and one big circle costs less per block covered than a cluster of small ones.
