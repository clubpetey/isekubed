# Build

A hand-held block placer. This circle is never drawn on a wall: you load it into a CAD, and firing the CAD places one block, picked at random from the blocks on your hotbar, against the face you aim at. Think of it as the reverse of the mine spell.

To copy a whole structure and build it again somewhere else, use [Blueprint]($circles/blueprint) instead.

It is a single level, and it costs mana each time you fire it.

| Circle | Mana (cast) |
|---|---|
| Build I | 200 |
