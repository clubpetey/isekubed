# Craft

Automated crafting. Draw it on a crafting table and right-click paper onto it to open the recipe scroll screen; the scroll you write is a single recipe. Bind that scroll to the circle and it crafts that recipe from the items dropped on it, or, when nothing is lying on it, from your storage network. Drawn on an ordinary block it waits for a scroll in the same way.

Results go into the block's inventory, or fall out below it. Pair it with [Smelt]($circles/smelt) on a mana block and a chain such as raw iron to ingots to blocks runs itself. A Craft circle on a chest also packs what it holds where it can, such as nine nuggets into an ingot.

Craft II crafts twice as fast as Craft I.

| Circle | Mana (cast) | Mana working (per batch) |
|---|---|---|
| Craft I | 200 | 20 |
| Craft II | 200 | 30 |
