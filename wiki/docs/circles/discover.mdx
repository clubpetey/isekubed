# Discover

The circle of study. Draw it on a pedestal inside a proper workshop and put a lesson scroll on the pedestal. Anyone holding a Magic Pen can then open it and work through the discovery puzzle that teaches the scroll's lesson.

Each glyph drawn, right or wrong, costs the circle's owner a little mana and the pen a little ink. If the owner's mana runs out, the attempt ends and you are told you are too tired to continue. The circle costs nothing while nobody is studying.

It has a single level.

| Circle | Mana (cast) | Mana working (per batch) |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | 200 | 80 |
