# Imbuement

The imbuement circles come in three kinds.

- **Self Imbuement** - make a potion effect permanent. Drink the potion, then stand on the circle: the first active effect that is not already imbued converts, and the circle is used up. It comes in three strengths: Waking lasts until you sleep or die, Life lasts until you die, and Eternal lasts forever. Waking and Life imbuements cost a little mana from your own pool to keep; if you run dry the effect lapses until you can pay again, and the imbuement is not lost. Eternal costs nothing to keep. The stronger ones cost far more to draw.
- **Item Imbuement** - powers the imbuing ritual on its host block. An item thrown onto the circle that matches an imbuing recipe is transformed after a short wait, and the circle pays the mana as the ritual runs. Some recipes need a higher level of the circle.
- **Land Imbuement** - a spell that blooms the ground around the block you aim at. Level 1 covers the area with flowers, mushrooms, fungi or coral to suit the ground. Level 2 adds a beehive with bees, or fish and a fishing rod if you are underwater. Level 3 turns the spot into a watered 9x9 farm sown with seeds. Level 4 is level 1 with saplings among the flowers.
