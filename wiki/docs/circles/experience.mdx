# Experience

A bank for your experience. Stand on a floor circle and your experience is drained into your network. Stand under a ceiling circle and it is paid back out into you, with the XP bar ticking down as it goes. It has a single level, and the transfer costs a little mana.

| Circle | Mana (cast) | Mana upkeep (per batch) | Mana working (per batch) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Experience I | 200 | 1 | 20 |
