# Dragon's Roar

Breathes a cone of fire from your mouth in the direction you are looking. The fire lingers, and anything alive inside the cone takes damage once a second, except you.

Higher levels widen the cone, lengthen it, make it linger longer and burn harder. By level IX the cone is a full circle. Costs soul. There is no Dragon's Roar I, the first level is II.
