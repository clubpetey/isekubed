# Smelt

Smelting without a furnace. Throw items on the circle and they smelt into the result, or draw it on a chest and it smelts what is inside. A creature standing on it can be smelted too where a recipe exists for it (never a player).

Ores smelted on a block that also carries a [Crush]($circles/crush) circle come out multiplied, and an enchanting circle set to Fortune on the same block adds Fortune on top. Raw iron, copper and gold take the Crush multiplier but not Fortune.

With a crafting scroll bound, it runs that one recipe. Level 1 runs furnace recipes and level 2 adds the blast furnace. Level 5 reaches a brewing stand, where a scroll records a sequence of brewing steps and the circle walks through them. Levels 3 and 4 are there for other mods to add their own stations to. Each level also works faster than the one below.

| Circle | Mana (cast) | Mana working (per batch) |

| Smelt III | 200 | 160 |
| Smelt IV | 200 | 200 |
