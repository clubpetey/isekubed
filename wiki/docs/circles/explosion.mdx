# Explosion

Detonates an explosion at the spot you aim at. The level sets the strength of the blast and nothing else: level II is as strong as a block of TNT and level X is five times that, while the mana cost is the same at every level. The radius figure in the table is that strength on vanilla's scale, where TNT is 4.

It hurts you if you are standing in it, so aim further away. Whether it breaks blocks is a server setting; mobs and players are hurt and thrown either way. Costs soul.
