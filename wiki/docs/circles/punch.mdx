# Punch

Hits whatever you aim at with massive damage and sends it flying. It is one target only, with no area; aimed at a block or at nothing it does nothing, but the mana is still spent.

Damage is 4 per level and the knockback grows with the level too. Costs soul. It is Gale's push with a weapon's edge on it.
