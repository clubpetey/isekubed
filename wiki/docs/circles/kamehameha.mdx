# Kamehameha

Channels a beam from your eyes that follows your crosshair for 10 seconds. Turn your head and the beam sweeps with you; anything in its path takes damage once a second. It stops at the first block, it spares tamed pets and pushes nothing, and no other spell can be cast while it runs. Pressing Esc ends it early.

Damage is 10 per level per hit, and the beam's range grows in steps with the level, from 5 blocks at I to 40 at X. Below level X the beam is spent on the first thing it hits; at level X it pierces, continuing through targets to the ones behind. Costs soul.
