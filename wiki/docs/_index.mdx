# Isekubed

**Draw a circle. Extend Minecraft. Become a god. File the paperwork.**

Isekubed is an all-in-one magic and utility mod built on three ideas: magic circles, choices that matter, and anime
references. One rune alphabet runs everything: the circles you draw on blocks, the enchantments you put on gear and the
spells you cast.

This wiki covers what things do and what they cost. It does not spell out circles or give away the paths you are
meant to discover. Finding those out is part of the game.
