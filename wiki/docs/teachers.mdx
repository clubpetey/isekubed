# Teachers

Teachers don't simply wander in: each one has to be called, and how is for you to find. Every teacher guards a
signature spell. Defeat one and the spell is yours. There is also a way to earn it without a fight.

## Other teachers

- **Riker Loot** teaches no spell, but his eye becomes the **Eye of Loot**, your window into everything else. See
  [Progression]($progression).
- **Deo** teaches **The End**, a Stand that stops time, and only to someone who already has a Stand. See
  [Stands]($mechanics/stands).
