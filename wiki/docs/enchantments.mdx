# Enchantments

Isekubed adds five enchantments. Like every enchantment in the mod, you choose them instead of rolling for them.

- **Life Steal** (weapons, I–X): heals you for 20% of the damage the victim actually takes, per level, after armour and
  other reductions. Works with bows too.
- **Wither** (weapons, I): a direct hit applies Wither II for 10 seconds.
- **Steadfast** (armour, I–X): +2.5% knockback resistance per level, per armour piece.
- **Invisible** (armour, I): the worn piece is not drawn at all, including its model, trim and glint.
- **Prima Materia** (I): an alchemical enchantment that belongs to the Great Work. What it is for is yours to discover.

| Enchantment | Max level | Applies to | Slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invisible | I | `#minecraft:enchantable/armor` | armor |
| Life Steal | X | `#isekubed:enchantable/all_weapons` | mainhand |
| Prima Materia | I | `minecraft:raw_copper` | mainhand |
| Steadfast | X | `#minecraft:enchantable/armor` | armor |
| Wither | I | `#isekubed:enchantable/all_weapons` | mainhand |
